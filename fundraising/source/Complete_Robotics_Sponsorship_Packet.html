About us

Complete Robotics is a community robotics team based in Frankenmuth. Our students are in grades 7 through 12 and come from public, private, virtual and home schools and the career center in Frankenmuth, Birch Run, Saginaw and Bay City. No experience is needed to join.

The team is part of Complete Worship's youth programs, which began in 2019 and added STEM programs in 2024.

About FIRST

FIRST Robotics Competition is an international program for high school students. Each January, teams get a new game and about eight weeks to design, build and program a robot of up to 125 pounds to play it. More than 93,000 students in 35 countries took part in the 2025 season.

Our first season, 2025–26

  • Reached the finals at both of our district events
  • Won the Rookie All Star Award
  • Qualified for and competed in the FIRST in Michigan State Championship

What students do

Students work in coding, CAD, building, machining, wiring, 3D printing, driving, scouting, media and marketing. Mentors with backgrounds in engineering, machining, electronics and programming guide them.

Our 2026 robot

Students designed the robot in CAD and programmed it in Java. It drove on a swerve drivetrain, where each wheel steers on its own, and launched balls with a flywheel shooter. We didn't have a way to machine metal, so we 3D printed many of the custom parts, including gears and the shooter frame.

This season's goal

This season we want to qualify for the FIRST Championship in Houston. Our biggest need is a CNC router so students can cut parts from aluminum instead of printing them. Aluminum parts are stronger and more precise, and the machine will serve the team for years.

The FIRST Championship fund covers registration and a travel deposit. If we qualify, we will raise travel money separately.

How to become a sponsor
