Sponsor Complete Robotics

We're a high school FIRST Robotics Competition team from Frankenmuth, now in our second season. As rookies, our robot made it to the Michigan State Championship. This year we're building to qualify for the FIRST Championship in Houston, and local sponsors are how we get there.

Built on a 3D printer

Students design every part in CAD and write all the code in Java. With no machine shop, every custom part was printed in plastic. A CNC router lets them cut parts like these from aluminum.

Where the money goes

The CNC router is the biggest single item. Parts are the biggest category: motors and electronics wear out over a season, and a Worlds-level robot needs spares.

The Worlds fund covers registration and a travel deposit. If we qualify, we'll run a separate drive to get the whole team to Houston.
