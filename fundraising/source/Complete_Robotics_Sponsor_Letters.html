Dear Mr. Grossi,

I'm writing on behalf of Complete Robotics, a community FIRST Robotics Competition team (Team 10978) based here in Frankenmuth. Our students are in grades 7 through 12 and come from Frankenmuth, Birch Run, Saginaw and Bay City.

Last season was our first. The students designed, built and programmed their own robot, reached the finals at both of our district events, won the Rookie All Star Award and qualified for the Michigan State Championship.

This season we are raising money for a CNC router. Last year we 3D printed many of our custom parts because we had no way to machine metal, and we want students to learn to make those parts from aluminum. Since Kremin does precision machining right here in town, we would be grateful for your help in whatever form works for you: a sponsorship, machine time for a few parts, or a visit where students could see how a professional shop runs.

I've enclosed our sponsorship packet, which lists our sponsorship levels and how sponsors are recognized. I'll follow up by phone in the next week or so. We would also be glad to bring the robot by so you can see it in person.

Thank you for considering our request.

Sincerely,


Sarah Rice
