Project summary

CNC workshop project

Attach to applications that ask for a project description or budget: Gene Haas Foundation, Hemlock Semiconductor CARE, Saginaw Community Foundation, Consumers Energy.

The need

In our 2026 rookie season, students designed a complete competition robot but had no way to machine metal. Every custom part was 3D-printed. Printed parts take longer to make, break more easily on impact, and limit what students can design. The robot still reached the finals at both district events and qualified for the State Championship. Our mentors include people with machining experience; what we lack is the machine. A CNC router is the biggest upgrade between us and a FIRST Championship robot.

The project

  • Buy and set up a benchtop CNC router (Omio X8 class, the most common router on FRC teams) with a stand, enclosure and dust collection.
  • Train students in the full process: design in CAD, program toolpaths, set up the machine safely, cut parts and check them against the drawing.
  • Build our 2027 robot with student-machined aluminum and polycarbonate parts, and offer CNC help to other local teams.

Budget

CNC router, Omio X8-2200L class (WCP lists it at $3,999.99)$4,000
Machine stand and enclosure$1,000
Dust collection$400
End mills, bits, collets and workholding$700
Spoilboard, safety equipment, wiring and setup$400
Total$6,500

What we'll report

  • Students trained on the CNC (goal: everyone on the build team)
  • Share of custom robot parts machined in-house (goal: most structural parts)
  • Competition results, including whether we qualify for the FIRST Championship
  • Students going on to engineering, manufacturing or skilled-trades programs
