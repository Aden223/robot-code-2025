Dear Members of the Board,

On behalf of Complete Robotics, I am requesting a grant of $2,500 to help purchase a CNC router for our team workshop.

Complete Robotics (FIRST Robotics Competition Team 10978) is a community team for students in grades 7 through 12, run as part of Complete Worship's youth programs in Frankenmuth. The router, enclosure and tooling will cost about $6,500. Your grant would cover more than a third of that cost, and we are seeking the balance from other local funders and sponsors. We would be glad to bring students and the robot to a board meeting.

CNC Router Project Summary

Complete Robotics, FRC Team 10978  ·  For use with grant applications

Need

In our 2025–26 rookie season, students designed and built a competition robot that reached the finals at both of our district events and qualified for the Michigan State Championship. Without a way to machine metal, they 3D printed many of the custom parts. Printed parts take longer to make, wear faster and limit what students can design. Our mentors have machining experience; what we need is the machine.

Project

  • Purchase and install a benchtop CNC router (Omio X8 class, a common choice for FIRST teams) with a stand, enclosure and dust collection.
  • Train students to design parts in CAD, create toolpaths, set up the machine safely, cut parts and check them against the drawing.
  • Build the 2027 robot with student-machined aluminum and polycarbonate parts.

Budget (estimated)

CNC router, Omio X8-2200L class (WCP lists it at $3,999.99)$4,000
Machine stand and enclosure$1,000
Dust collection$400
End mills, collets and workholding$700
Spoilboard, safety equipment, wiring and setup$400
Total$6,500

What we will report

  • Number of students trained on the machine
  • Share of the robot's custom parts made in-house
  • Competition results for the 2026–27 season
  • Students who go on to study engineering, manufacturing or the skilled trades
